# Bundled artwork

- RAEELogo: user-supplied original circular RAEE-INDUSTRIES emblem, used without redesign.
- GoogleLogo: Google G icon downloaded from https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/googleg/2x/googleg_standard_color_128dp.png.
- YouTube and navigation controls: native SF Symbols.
- GlassRim and MountainLake: newly generated bitmap assets, bundled locally. Built-in image generation was used. The rim uses an alpha channel and native nine-slice stretching; it is not a static image of a working browser.

GlassRim prompt: Empty straight-on faceted clear teal/cyan crystal-glass rim, landscape rectangle, four small brushed titanium screw caps in rounded corners, narrow luminous optical border, transparent centre and outside, no screen, UI, words, logo, camera or stand.

MountainLake prompt: Full-bleed photorealistic teal alpine mountain lake at night, mirror reflection, forested slopes and central valley, dark starry sky above, soft cyan twilight at the horizon, no buildings, people, UI, frame, logo or words; landscape composition usable cropped to portrait.
